# Salon Promotion Starter Kit — Unpublished Product Draft

**Proposed price:** $29 one-time. **Status:** draft only; not for sale yet.

## Customer promise
Ready-to-customize marketing prompts, caption starters, and a practical four-week salon promotion calendar, designed for independent beauty professionals.

## Contents
- 12 editable caption templates (new-client welcome, rebooking reminder, seasonal color, haircut refresh, stylist spotlight, retail education, referral appreciation, appointment openings, consultation, review request, gift card, and client FAQ).
- 4 weekly posting schedules with suggested themes and calls to action.
- Plain-language instructions for customizing offers and removing unsupported claims.

## Sample caption
**Appointment openings:** 'A few openings are available this week. Want a fresh look or a little maintenance? Send us a message or use our booking link to find a time that works for you. [Insert booking URL]'

## Week 1 sample plan
Monday: introduce the team; Wednesday: explain one popular service; Friday: share booking availability (only if true).
